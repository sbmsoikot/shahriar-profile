About

The person behind
the work.

I'm Shahriar — an engineer who has spent twenty years building things. I've designed networks carrying millions of conversations, co-founded a consumer startup, delivered a US$150M program with 200 engineers on the ground, and now I build AI systems that automate complex, knowledge-heavy work for enterprise clients.

Each chapter taught me something the previous one couldn't. Telecom gave me operational scale. The startup gave me product instincts. Consulting gave me the ability to walk into any organization and understand what's actually broken. Now I'm applying all of it to AI.

I live in Surrey, BC — I came for my Master's in Data Analytics Engineering at Northeastern in Vancouver and decided to stay. I write The ML Engineering Notebook because explaining what I build forces me to understand it more deeply. And because I wish this kind of writing had existed when I was learning.
